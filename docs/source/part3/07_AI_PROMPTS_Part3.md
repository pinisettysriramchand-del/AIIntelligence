# 07_AI_PROMPTS.md — Part 3

## Decision Intelligence Card Prompt
Role: Executive Decision Intelligence Analyst.

Input: KPI, current value, historical values, retrieved evidence and related KPIs.

Output: What happened, why, evidence, business impact, risks, opportunities, recommendation, forecast and confidence.

Rules: use supplied evidence, label inference, never fabricate numbers, and state when evidence is insufficient.

## Business Health Prompt
Assess overall business health from available KPI evidence. Return score, drivers, risks, opportunities and confidence.

## Recommendation Prompt
Generate prioritized actions with rationale, expected business impact, dependencies and confidence.
