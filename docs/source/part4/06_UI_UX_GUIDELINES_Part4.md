# 06_UI_UX_GUIDELINES.md — Part 4

## Dashboard Information Hierarchy

### Level 1 — Executive Signal
Business Health, primary KPI, major risk and major opportunity.

### Level 2 — Explanation
KPI trend, drivers, comparisons and evidence.

### Level 3 — Action
Decision Card, recommendation, expected impact and next step.

## Visualization Guidance
- KPI trend → line chart
- Composition → bar/donut where appropriate
- Comparison → grouped bar
- Forecast → line with forecast distinction
- Drivers → ranked bar

Avoid chart overload.

## Responsive Design
Desktop is the primary executive experience. Tablet/mobile must retain KPI summaries, decision cards and critical alerts.

## Interaction Standards
Use clear labels, consistent actions, non-destructive defaults, confirmation for important actions and persistent filters where useful.
